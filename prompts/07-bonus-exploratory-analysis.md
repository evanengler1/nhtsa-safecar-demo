# Bonus — Exploratory Analysis Prompts

These are standalone prompts participants can try at any point to explore the data and CoCo's capabilities. Pick any one:

```
Using the NHTSA_SAFECAR_DEMO.SAFETY_DATA data, answer one of the following and visualize the result as a chart:

- Which vehicle manufacturers have improved their average overall safety rating the most between 2015 and 2024? Show before/after ratings and the improvement, filtered to brands with at least 10 vehicles in each period.
- Using the SAFECAR_SAFETY_RATINGS semantic view, what are the top 10 safest SUVs by overall rating from 2020 or later?
- What is the year-over-year growth rate of automatic emergency braking (AEB) adoption across all vehicles?
- Compare the average head injury criterion (HIC15_DRIV) between sedans, SUVs, and pickup trucks. Which body style has the lowest average driver head injury risk? Show it as a horizontal bar chart.
- Is there a correlation between vehicle curb weight and rollover probability? Calculate the correlation coefficient and show a scatter plot colored by body style.
- What percentage of tested vehicles received a 5-star overall safety rating each year? Show the trend as a line chart.
```
